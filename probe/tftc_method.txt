class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">What the data covers</h2><div class="mt-4 space-y-4 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><p>One row per US trading session for every US spot Bitcoin ETF, starting with the first session on January 11, 2024. Each row carries the net flow across all the funds in US dollars, a per-fund breakdown, the funds&#x27; combined total net assets and a reference BTC/USD price. A net flow is the dollar value of shares created minus shares redeemed; a negative number is an outflow. A row with a net flow of zero and no per-fund split is treated as a market holiday.</p><p>Bitcoin futures ETFs, funds listed outside the US and companies holding bitcoin on their balance sheets are not included.</p></div></section><section id="sources" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">Sources</h2><dl class="mt-5 space-y-5 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Vendor aggregates</dt><dd class="mt-1">The vendor aggregate, total net assets and value traded come from<!-- --> <a href="https://sosovalue.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-bitcoin hover:text-oraper">SoSoValue</a>. The sync calls SoSoValue&#x27;s official Open API with TFTC&#x27;s key and falls back to the keyless endpoint SoSoValue&#x27;s own dashboard uses when the keyed call fails. Each call returns roughly the last 300 sessions.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Per-fund flows, July 2026 on</dt><dd class="mt-1">SoSoValue&#x27;s keyed per-fund endpoint, which serves about the last month for each fund. The list of funds is read from SoSoValue on every run, so a newly listed fund appears without a code change.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Per-fund history, launch to mid-July 2026</dt><dd class="mt-1">Issuer disclosures as tabulated by<!-- --> <a href="https://farside.co.uk/bitcoin-etf-flow-all-data/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-bitcoin hover:text-oraper">Farside Investors</a>, loaded once from an archived copy of Farside&#x27;s table. The sessions from July 17 to July 24, 2026 were filled by hand from an archived snapshot before the keyed per-fund sync took over.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">BTC/USD reference price</dt><dd class="mt-1"><a href="https://mempool.space" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-bitcoin hover:text-oraper">mempool.space</a>&#x27;s historical price nearest 21:00 UTC on each trading day, a reference mark for the cumulative chart.</dd></div></dl></section><section id="schedule" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">Update schedule</h2><div class="mt-4 space-y-4 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><p>The sync runs every day at 02:00 and 10:00 UTC. The evening run lands the session that just closed, after issuers report; the morning run picks up late filings and revisions. Every run re-reads SoSoValue&#x27;s aggregate window of about 300 sessions. Per-fund reports are checked within the keyed API&#x27;s most recent 30 days.</p><p>After each sync the hub, the current and previous month pages, any older month a revision touched and both JSON files are regenerated. Between syncs the hub is regenerated on a 30-minute window and the month pages on an hourly one.</p></div></section><section id="merging" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">How the sources are combined</h2><div class="mt-4 space-y-4 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><p>Total is the sum of the listed fund flows before rounding. The historical fund values come from issuer disclosures as tabulated by Farside Investors. When no breakdown is available, Total uses the vendor aggregate. Vendor aggregates may differ from the fund sum because of coverage, rounding or revisions. Both JSON files retain the vendor aggregate as<code>vendorNetFlowUsd</code>; <code>netFlowUsd</code> is the Total used throughout the tracker. Statistics, charts and monthly and cumulative figures all use that same Total.</p><p>The sync refreshes vendor aggregates and preserves the Farside archive. Within the most recent 30 days, a first complete SoSoValue breakdown is accepted when its sum is within $5 million of the vendor aggregate. Replacing an accepted report requires every fund in its stored map to report and the new sum to be within $0.5 million of the fresh aggregate. A changed ticker list or an incomplete fetch does not withdraw an accepted session. Gaps over $0.5 million are logged without failing the run. Null or missing fund reports are unreported, not zero. Known incomplete sessions stay out of public figures until a complete report is accepted. Missing BTC prices are looked up again for the last 30 days on every run.</p></div></section><section id="computed" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">How the figures are computed</h2><dl class="mt-5 space-y-5 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Cumulative net flow</dt><dd class="mt-1">The sum of every session&#x27;s net flow since January 11, 2024.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Total net assets</dt><dd class="mt-1">The figure from the most recent session that reports one.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Streak</dt><dd class="mt-1">Consecutive sessions with net flows in the same direction, counted back from the newest session. Zero-flow rows are skipped: they neither extend nor break a streak.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Records</dt><dd class="mt-1">The biggest single-session inflow and outflow across the whole series, and the best and worst calendar months by summed net flow.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">This week and month to date</dt><dd class="mt-1">This week runs from Monday through the newest session. Month to date covers the newest session&#x27;s calendar month.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Per-fund totals</dt><dd class="mt-1">Each fund&#x27;s daily flows summed over the window shown: the latest session, the past month (the last 21 rows of the daily series, holiday rows included), the year to date (the newest session&#x27;s calendar year) and since launch. Sessions still waiting for their per-fund split are left out of fund totals, and a month page says so when that happens.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Month pages</dt><dd class="mt-1">Trading days count the sessions with a non-zero flow or a per-fund split. The biggest day is the session with the largest move in either direction; the daily average is the month&#x27;s net flow divided by its trading days.</dd></div></dl></section><section id="dates" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">Update dates</h2><div class="mt-4 space-y-4 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><p>Every page and file states one update date, taken from the data: the newest session it shows, stamped at <!-- -->22:00 UTC<!-- --> <!-- -->that day, after the 4:00 pm Eastern close in both summer and winter time. The same value is the page&#x27;s structured-data dateModified, its sitemap lastmod and the dateModified in both JSON files, where updatedThrough gives the bare session date. It moves only when a new session arrives.</p><p>The hub also shows when the sync last checked for revisions. That is the time of the last run, which can be later than the last change, so it is labelled as a check.</p></div></section><section id="limitations" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">Known limitations</h2><dl class="mt-5 space-y-5 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">The newest session can move</dt><dd class="mt-1">Issuers report after the close and some smaller funds report a day late, so the latest total can change on the next sync.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Revisions older than the window</dt><dd class="mt-1">SoSoValue&#x27;s window covers about 300 sessions. A revision to an older session is not picked up automatically.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Price gaps</dt><dd class="mt-1">A BTC price missing for more than 30 days stays missing until it is backfilled by hand.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">A day behind the market</dt><dd class="mt-1">Flows are reported after the US close, so the data always runs at least one session behind live trading.</dd></div><div><dt class="font-display text-xl font-semibold text-ivory">Hashdex&#x27;s DEFI</dt><dd class="mt-1">DEFI has a daily table column and counts in the fund sum and per-fund leaderboard. Every available ticker is displayed, including newly listed funds.</dd></div></dl></section><section id="corrections" class="mt-12 max-w-3xl scroll-mt-24"><h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-semibold text-ivory">Corrections</h2><div class="mt-4 space-y-4 font-serif text-lg leading-relaxed text-ivory/75"><p>Vendor aggregates refresh within SoSoValue&#x27;s aggregate window. Recent SoSoValue reports use the 30 day window, a $5 million first acceptance gate and a $0.5 million revision gate. An accepted report keeps its stored fund cohort. Farside archive fund values are preserved.
